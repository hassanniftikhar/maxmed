02

The verdict

Short version. We are not losing the island-dressing shopper. Our hero has lost the generic shopper – people searching "large bandages", "bandages", "wound care & dressings", "gauze pads". Purchases from those searches fell 38% from July to September (1,956 → 1,207), while purchases from island / bordered / branded searches stayed flat (~930–1,100 a month since March). At the same time a cheaper competitor, YHOVID, took the Sponsored Brands banner on "island dressing" (our share 83% → 5%) and drove top-of-search clicks to $9–15 in mid-September. Our campaigns, all set to "down only" bidding and spread over 22 campaigns per head term, could not respond.
The fix on ads

Rebuild the family at child level – one owner campaign per keyword, 1 ad group and ≤6 keywords per campaign, fixed bids on the terms that matter, and a bid for every keyword calculated from its own revenue per click.

The fix on the head term

Put more of the head-term fight where it costs us the least ACOS: Sponsored Brands (27% ACOS on "island dressing"), the 6x6 and 60ct, and competitor pages. On "island dressing" one click on a 6x6 ad brings in $8.65 of sales and one click on a 60ct ad $10.27, vs $5.83 for the hero (sales ÷ clicks, SP Search Term report) – they sell for more, so they can pay a higher CPC and still stay under their ACOS target.

The ACOS plan

Same monthly spend (~$47.5k across SP + SB + SD). Planned blended ACOS target 40.7% vs 44.0% today; SP alone 43.0% vs 49.0% today. Today’s ACOS is the ceiling we manage to throughout.

What we know, and what we don't

What we know
  • The family sells ~$9.4–10.5k a day – about 31% of the account's sales (Business Reports).
  • Family ad spend is ~$47.5k a month: SP ~$38.5k, SB ~$9.1k, SD $0 (22 Aug–18 Oct, scaled to 30.4 days).
  • Current ACOS: SP 49.0%, SB 30.7%, blended 44.0%. TACOS ≈ 16% (family sales ≈ $9.6k/day).
  • The hero's loss is generic traffic, not island-dressing traffic (SQP ASIN view).
  • YHOVID is the "$10 for 50" competitor: #1 organic and Amazon's Choice on "island dressing", ~$0.18 per pad vs our $0.33 (Helium 10).
  • Exactly how the campaigns are built, bid and modified (bulk file).
What we don't know yet
  • Hourly performance – needed for dayparting. Scale Insights starts collecting from the day it is connected.
  • Exact dates and terms of the bid war from the in-house team.
  • How the +60–100% Amazon Business placement modifier performs – the placement report does not split it out.
  • Why the hero's main category flipped to Industrial & Scientific on 19 Jun and back on 13 Oct.
  • Inventory depth per size for Q4 – we will not scale a size into a stock-out.
  • Promotions planned (the hero's average selling price fell $9.97 → $9.51 with a flat $9.98 list price).
  • COGS – by agreement we plan on ACOS only.

The strategy and the rules behind it

Why child level, not parent level

The sizes behave like different products. On the same search term "island dressing", one click on a hero ad brings in $5.83 of sales (73% ACOS), on a 6x6 ad $8.65 (37%), on a 60ct ad $10.27 (51%) – SP Search Term report, single-size campaigns. We call this revenue per click (sales ÷ clicks) throughout this document. A parent-level campaign lets Amazon pick the size and averages these together – that is exactly what the legacy B09M7PDXTM campaigns do today. So every keyword campaign advertises one child, sits in that child's portfolio, and carries that child's ACOS target. Parent level is used only where it helps: Sponsored Brands collections and own-page defense.

How every bid is set

Max CPC = target ACOS × revenue per click. Revenue per click comes from the keyword's own last 58 days (sales ÷ clicks), using the advertised size's own campaigns when it has ≥ 10 clicks, otherwise the family's. The base bid is then max CPC ÷ (1 + top-of-search modifier), so even a top-of-search click cannot cost more than the ACOS target allows. Terms with too little data start at the campaign's median revenue per click. Launch cap: no base bid starts more than 25% above today's CPC (marked ↑cap); it is raised later only if the keyword stays under its ACOS target.

Example: hero "island dressing" – $5.83 revenue per click × 55% = $3.20 max CPC; with +50% TOS the base bid is $2.14. Today the hero pays $4.25 per click on it.
